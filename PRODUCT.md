## Product Purpose

Clovis Web Design is a modern web engineering and search/AI marketing studio that helps businesses grow their online visibility, leads, and revenue. We offer five distinct, unbundled lifecycle services—Brand Positioning & Conversion Copy, Custom High-Performance Web Design, Search Engine & Google Maps Optimization (SEO), AI Search & Answer Engine Optimization (GEO for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Siri), and AI/Search Lead Attribution & Retention—each scoped to the client's goals, market, and ROI.

## Positioning

An authoritative, modern web engineering and online marketing partner based in Clovis, California and serving businesses across California and the US. Clients work directly with the engineer (Adam) with zero account-manager middlemen, sub-second (`<0.4s`) static code that AI crawlers never time out on, verifiable 2026 search and AI citation architecture, and 100% client ownership of their website assets.

## Operating Context

- **Modern Search Reality (2026)**: Customers no longer just click through ten blue links on Google—they check the Google Maps 3-Pack or ask ChatGPT, Google Gemini (AI Overviews), Claude, Perplexity, and Apple Siri who to hire. Each engine uses a different data source ("phonebook"), and traditional WordPress/Wix templates fail because they time out on AI bot crawls (`>1s`) or lack structured entity data.
- **Commercial Stakes**: Every service must clearly articulate *why it matters* to the business's bottom line (more calls, higher conversion rates, qualified referrals, and measurable ROI).
- **Scoped to Value & ROI**: Instead of one-size-fits-all retail price stickers, engagements are scoped to what the business actually needs to win its market—whether that is a standalone Search & AI Visibility overhaul on an existing website, a custom-engineered website build, or ongoing monthly Search & AI growth.
- **Direct Engineer Access**: Clients communicate directly with the builder and strategist via phone, SMS, or email rather than navigating agency ticket queues.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack**: Astro 5 (SSG/SSR), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, TypeScript, deployed to Vercel's global edge network.
- **Performance Targets**: Under 0.4s First Contentful Paint (FCP) for AI crawler timeout immunity, under 1.0s Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), 0.0 Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), 100/100 Google PageSpeed Mobile score, and full Chrome Lighthouse Agentic Browsing compliance (`/llms.txt`, `/.well-known/ai-catalog.json`, Declarative WebMCP, and Accessibility Tree integrity).
- **Plain Language**: Say what the thing is and *why it matters* commercially. No figures of speech or agricultural/orchard metaphors ("hand-grown", "harvest", "seedling", "farm stand" are banned). Use clear, authoritative agency language.
- **Client Terms**: True website ownership—clients own their domain, GitHub source code, and hosting from day one with zero "hostage fees" just to keep a website online. Ongoing monthly retainers are reserved for active Search, Maps, and AI Marketing growth that produces measurable results.
