# Agency Engineering & White-Label Partnering

Domain terminology for positioning Clovis Web Design as an overflow frontend engineering and white-label development partner for digital agencies.

## Language

**Agency Partner**:
A digital marketing, creative, or web design agency that subcontracts frontend engineering or web development to an outside specialist.
_Avoid_: Client, Customer, Buyer

**White-Label Build**:
A website or web application developed on behalf of an agency partner where the engineering contractor remains invisible to the end client.
_Avoid_: Freelance gig, Subcontract work, Outsource job

**Figma-to-Code**:
The exact, drift-free translation of Figma design mockups, autolayout, design tokens, and interactions into production-grade frontend code (Astro, React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript).
_Avoid_: Slicing, PSD to HTML, Template tweaking

**Dev Overflow**:
Excess development workload that an agency's in-house engineering team cannot absorb due to deadline constraints, capacity limits, or lack of modern performance engineering expertise.
_Avoid_: Extra work, Side work, Overflow backlog

**Production SLA**:
A defined commitment on turnaround time (e.g., 4–5 business days), QA benchmarks (100/100 Core Web Vitals, 0.0 CLS, WCAG 2.1 AA), and communication cadence.
_Avoid_: Deadline, Delivery window
