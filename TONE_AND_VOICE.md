## 3. How Modern Search & AI Work (The Verified 2026 Messaging Truth)

### A. The "4 Different Phonebooks" Reality
When explaining why a business can have good Google reviews yet remain invisible in ChatGPT, Claude, or Siri, explain the four distinct retrieval pipelines in plain English:
- **Google Gemini & AI Overviews:** Checks **Google Search (Top 20) + Google Business Profile (Maps) + matching `LocalBusiness` website schema + recent review velocity**.
- **ChatGPT (`OAI-SearchBot`):** Does **not** use Google Maps. Checks **Bing Places + Yelp + third-party directories** and crawls your website directly with a strict `<1s` timeout budget (pages loading in `<0.4s` get **3.2× more citations** than slow WordPress sites).
- **Claude (`ClaudeBot` + Brave Search):** Uses **Brave Search's independent index (86.7% overlap)** and reads **raw static HTML** without clicking JavaScript toggles or tabs. Visitors clicking from Claude convert at **16.8%** (nearly 8× higher than traditional search).
- **Apple Siri & Apple Intelligence:** Queries **Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps) + Yelp** on every iPhone.

### B. When the Website Alone Wins vs. When Website + Profiles Work Together
- **For specific buyer questions** (*"How much does X cost?"*, *"Who serves [neighborhood / zip code]?"*, *"Which practice takes direct referrals?"*) and for **ChatGPT / Claude web crawls**: **Your website's speed (`<0.4s`) and specific factual copy directly win the AI recommendation.** Maps profiles don't store detailed service breakdowns, pricing logic, zip codes, or workflows—the AI *must* extract that answer from a fast, clear website.
- **For broad directory lookups** (*"Who is the best [service] in [city]?"*): AI cross-checks your **Google Maps, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, and reviews against your website's structured schema**—which is why our Search & AI Visibility service aligns both your website code and your off-site profiles.
