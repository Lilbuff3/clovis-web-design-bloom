# Clovis Web Design

Domain language for Clovis Web Design (`cloviswebdesign.com`) — Adam's brand positioning, custom web engineering, Google Maps SEO, AI search optimization (GEO), and lead attribution studio.

## Studio & Engagement Model

**Lifecycle Pillar**:
One of the five core growth capabilities offered individually or combined: (01) Brand Positioning & Conversion Copy, (02) Custom Web Design & Engineering, (03) Search Engine & Google Maps SEO, (04) AI Search & Answer Optimization, and (05) AI & Search Lead Attribution & Retention.
_Avoid_: Service tier, Feature box, Add-on widget

**Engagement Model**:
One of the three ways work is scoped to a business's market and ROI: `Custom Website Build`, `Search & AI Visibility Setup`, and `Ongoing Search & AI Growth`.
_Avoid_: Retail plan, Price tier, Fixed sticker package

**Working Preview**:
A live, functional website prototype built on the business's real copy after a 15-minute discovery call and tested on a phone before any payment is owed.
_Avoid_: Mockup, Wireframe, Deposit phase

## AI Search, Discovery & Lead Capture

**Discovery Manifest**:
One of the machine-readable files generated from canonical studio data so search engines and AI answer engines can verify business identity, routes, and capabilities (`/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/.well-known/ai-catalog.json`).
_Avoid_: Static text dump, Hand-edited seo file

**Visibility Audit**:
A live, Google Maps–grounded Gemini lookup (`/api/ask-gemini/`) that asks the exact buyer question for a business's trade and city (`"I need a <trade> in <city>. Who do you recommend?"`) and reports whether the business was named alongside the cited Google Maps sources.
_Avoid_: SEO score, Mock scan, Keyword checker

**Personalized Audit Link**:
A deep link (`/?biz=...&trade=...&city=...&autorun=1#check`) sent in 1-to-1 outreach that scrolls directly to the Visibility Audit module and runs a live check for the recipient's business on page load.
_Avoid_: Campaign URL, Tracking pixel link

**Lead Alert**:
A real-time notification dispatched from an Edge endpoint (`/api/contact` or `/api/ask-gemini/`) to `ntfy.sh` or a JSON webhook when a prospect submits an inquiry or runs a Visibility Audit.
_Avoid_: Email blast, CRM sync

## Agency Engineering & White-Label Partnering

**Agency Partner**:
A digital marketing, creative, or web design agency that subcontracts frontend engineering or web development to an outside specialist.
_Avoid_: Client, Customer, Buyer

**White-Label Build**:
A website or web application developed on behalf of an agency partner where the engineering contractor remains invisible to the end client.
_Avoid_: Freelance gig, Subcontract work, Outsource job

**Figma-to-Code**:
The exact, drift-free translation of Figma design mockups, autolayout, design tokens, and interactions into production-grade frontend code (Astro, React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript).
_Avoid_: Slicing, PSD to HTML, Template tweaking

**Dev Overflow**:
Excess development workload that an agency's in-house engineering team cannot absorb due to deadline constraints, capacity limits, or lack of modern performance engineering expertise.
_Avoid_: Extra work, Side work, Overflow backlog

**Production SLA**:
A defined commitment on turnaround time, QA benchmarks (100/100 Core Web Vitals, 0.0 CLS, WCAG 2.1 AA), and communication cadence.
_Avoid_: Deadline, Delivery window
